## Overview

I build **AI systems that work in production** — agentic workflows, retrieval pipelines you can trust, and backends that don't fall over. Not demos, not wrappers around ChatGPT: deployed systems solving real problems in healthcare, legal, and education.

**What I build:**

🤖 **Agentic AI Systems** — multi-step agents with tool-calling, memory, and graceful fallbacks using **LangGraph** and modern orchestration patterns. I design the guardrails: execution traces, human checkpoints, verification steps — so agents are reliable enough to ship.

🔗 **MCP Servers & LLM Integrations** — certified in MCP development. I connect LLMs directly to your databases, internal tools, and APIs through secure, standardized Model Context Protocol servers.

📄 **RAG Pipelines That Don't Hallucinate** — hybrid retrieval combining vector search (**ChromaDB/Pinecone**), BM25 keyword matching, and Cohere reranking. Citation-grounded answers across 100k+ page document libraries. If your domain is legal, medical, or technical, accuracy isn't optional — it's the product.

⚙️ **FastAPI Production Backends** — async APIs, data pipelines, conversational systems with memory, deployed on Render/HuggingFace. Clean architecture your team can maintain.

🌐 **High-Volume Data Extraction** — Playwright-based async scrapers that handle Cloudflare-protected targets; I've built extraction systems for India's largest real-estate platform.

🧠 **LLM Fine-Tuning** — QLoRA training pipelines when retrieval isn't enough and your domain needs a model that speaks its language natively.
