# 🚀 Social Media Post Crafting Skill Suite (`posts`)

> **Outcome-driven, platform-optimized social media post generation for AI agents & manual posting.**

---

## 📌 Overview

The `posts` skill suite is a collection of modular **Agentic AI Skills** (`SKILL.md`) designed to transform engineering updates, project releases, and case studies into high-converting posts for **LinkedIn**, **Twitter/X**, and **Reddit**.

Each skill guides AI agents (such as Antigravity or OpenCode) to format posts according to platform-specific character limits, truncation rules, engagement structures, and current trending topics.

## 🛠️ Included Skills Breakdown

### 1. 💼 LinkedIn Skill (`linkedin/SKILL.md`)
* **Trigger Name**: `linkedin-post-crafting`
* **Rule Highlights**:
  * **"See More" Fold Optimization**: First 140 characters must feature a bold, trend-aligned hook.
  * **Outcome-Driven**: Highlights deliverables (hours saved, zero-hallucination accuracy) over technical jargon.
  * **Format**: Bulleted outcomes + direct CTA (`neural.arun.dev@gmail.com`) + 3–5 industry hashtags.

### 2. 🐦 Twitter / X Skill (`twitter/SKILL.md`)
* **Trigger Name**: `twitter-post-crafting`
* **Rule Highlights**:
  * **Strict Character Counting**: Single tweets capped at **280 characters** (URLs = 23 chars, Emojis = 2 chars).
  * **Thread Architecture**: Numbered threads (`1/`, `2/`, `3/`) with Hook + Promise in Tweet 1, deliverables in middle tweets, and CTA in final tweet.

### 3. 🔴 Reddit Skill (`reddit/SKILL.md`)
* **Trigger Name**: `reddit-post-crafting`
* **Rule Highlights**:
  * **Community Posts** (`r/JEENEETards`, `r/IndianMedSchool`, `r/Python`): Value-first title (60-80 chars optimal), authentic student/developer story, free tools, open discussion prompt.
  * **Hiring Posts** (`r/forhire`, `r/hiring`): `[FOR HIRE]` title tag format with rate, executive summary, outcomes, and proof links.

---

## 🔥 Mandatory Step 0: Trend Alignment

All skills require AI agents to execute **Step 0** before drafting:
1. Search current platform trends, hot subreddit discussions, or viral tech debates.
2. Align the post hook with the active trend to maximize reach and retention.
