# 💼 LinkedIn Post Crafting Skill

## 🎯 Goal
Transform technical updates, project releases, case studies, and engineering insights into **high-converting, outcome-driven LinkedIn posts** aligned with **current trending industry topics**.

---

## 🔥 STEP 0: Trend Search & Angle Alignment (MANDATORY)

Before drafting any post, the agent MUST:
1. **Search Current Industry Trends**: Search for trending news, viral debates, or active discussions in AI, Healthcare, EdTech, or Developer tooling.
2. **Hook Into the Trend**: Connect Arun's project/outcome to the active trending topic in line 1-2.

---

## 📏 1. Platform Limits & Rules (2026)

* **Maximum Character Limit**: **3,000 characters** per post.
* **"See More" Truncation Fold**:
  * **Mobile**: Cut off at **~140 characters** (first 2 lines).
  * **Desktop**: Cut off at **210–250 characters**.
  * **Rule**: Your hook MUST fit within the first 140 characters and leverage a trending topic/angle. Do NOT put blank lines at the top.
* **Optimal Post Length**: **1,300 to 1,900 characters** for maximum algorithm reach.
* **Hashtag Limit**: Exactly **3–5 relevant hashtags** at the very bottom.

---

## 🎨 2. Bold Text & Visual Styling Rules (CRITICAL)

* **Strategic Bold Formatting**: Use `**bold text**` on key metrics, core value phrases, and section headers to make posts instantly scannable.
* **Where to Use Bold Text**:
  - **Key Metrics & Outcomes**: `**17,735+ NCERT MCQs**`, `**30-second test reviews**`, `**100% grounded accuracy**`.
  - **Core System/Project Names**: `**NEET AI Diagnostic Ecosystem**`, `**Zero-Hallucination RAG Engine**`.
  - **Category Anchors**: `**For Students**`, `**For Institutes & Founders**`, `**What It Delivers**`.

---

## 📐 3. Section-by-Section Writing Guide

### Section 1: The Trending Hook (Lines 1-2 | Under 140 Characters)
* Must tie into a **current industry trend, news, or common debate**.
* *Example*: `With recent updates in AI diagnostic tools, handing a NEET student a 450/720 score card without telling them **WHICH NCERT line** they missed is outdated. So I fixed it.`

### Section 2: The Problem & Context (Lines 3-5)
* Share the real friction point from first-hand experience (e.g. NEET prep struggle, slow clinical workflows, manual PDF research).

### Section 3: The Outcome & Deliverables (Body)
* Use clear bullet points with icon anchors (`🩺`, `📚`, `📄`, `⚡`, `📊`).
* Use **bold text** for key deliverables and numbers.

### Section 4: Call to Action (CTA) & Links
* Direct, frictionless invitation:
  * *For Students/Users*: Free link or trial.
  * *For Clients/Institutes*: "DM me or email `neural.arun.dev@gmail.com` to build this for your team."

### Section 5: Trending Hashtags
* `#AI #EdTech #Healthcare #Python #BuildInPublic`
