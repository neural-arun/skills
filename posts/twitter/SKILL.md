# 🐦 Twitter / X Post & Thread Crafting Skill

## 🎯 Goal
Transform technical projects, build updates, and engineering lessons into **high-retention single tweets or multi-tweet threads** aligned with **trending X/tech topics**.

---

## 🔥 STEP 0: Trend Search & Viral Hook Alignment (MANDATORY)

Before drafting any tweet or thread, the agent MUST:
1. **Search X/Tech Trends**: Check for trending hashtags, viral tech debates, open-source AI news, or current developer memes.
2. **Anchor the Hook to the Trend**: Start Tweet 1 with a hook that piggybacks on what people are actively reading and discussing on X right now.

---

## 📏 1. Platform Limits & Counting Rules (2026)

* **Free Account Limit**: Strictly **280 characters** per tweet.
* **URL Counting Rule**: Every link/URL counts as **exactly 23 characters** regardless of actual length.
* **Emoji Counting Rule**: Most emojis count as **2 characters** each.
* **Images/Videos**: Media attachments **do not** consume character count.
* **Thread Rule**: EVERY individual tweet inside a thread MUST be under 280 characters.

---

## 🎨 2. Bold Text & Visual Styling Rules (CRITICAL)

* **Strategic Bold Formatting**: Use `**bold text**` on key metrics, project names, and takeaways to grab attention on fast-moving Twitter feeds.
* **Where to Use Bold Text**:
  - **Key Metrics**: `**17,735+ NCERT MCQs**`, `**30-second reviews**`, `**0% hallucination**`.
  - **Core System/Project Names**: `**NEET AI Diagnostic Engine**`, `**Legal RAG System**`.
  - **Thread Anchors**: `**The Problem:**`, `**The Solution:**`, `**Tech Stack:**`.

---

## 📐 3. Section-by-Section Writing Guide

### A. Single Tweet Format (Under 280 Characters)
* **Line 1**: Trend-aligned hook with **bold highlights** (under 100 chars).
* **Line 2**: Outcome / Fix.
* **Line 3**: Link (23 chars) + CTA.
* *Example*:  
  `Everyone is talking about AI tutors, but most test series hand NEET candidates score cards with **ZERO feedback**.`  
  `So I built an **AI practice bot** with instant weak-area PDF diagnostics.`  
  `17,735+ NCERT MCQs (Free): t.me/gptbiologybot 🚀`

---

### B. Multi-Tweet Thread Format (For Major Builds & Engineering Stories)

#### Tweet 1 (The Trend Hook): Max 280 Characters
* Must connect a **trending topic/debate** to your project with **bold key terms** and end with `🧵👇`.

#### Tweet 2 (The Friction Point): Max 280 Characters
* **The Problem**: Explain the exact pain point in 2-3 short sentences.

#### Tweet 3 (The Solution & Deliverables): Max 280 Characters
* **The Solution**: List metrics (`**17.7k+ MCQs**`, `**1-min CBT countdowns**`, `**AI PDF reports**`).

#### Tweet 4 (The Tech Stack): Max 280 Characters
* **Tech Stack**: Python · FastAPI · RAG · LangChain · Telegram API · Vercel.

#### Tweet 5 (The Call to Action): Max 280 Characters
* **Try It / CTA**: Direct links & contact info (`github.com/neural-arun`, `neural.arun.dev@gmail.com`).
