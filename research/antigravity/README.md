## 🧮 14-Dimension Quantitative Evaluation Framework

Every problem identified in this study was rigorously evaluated across **14 quantitative dimensions** (scored 1–10):

1. **Pain Severity (1–10)**: Legal, financial, or operational impact when the workflow fails.
2. **Frequency (1–10)**: Daily/weekly volume vs sporadic seasonal peaks.
3. **Economic Cost (1–10)**: Annual institution labor spend + compliance/litigation penalties.
4. **Buyer Willingness to Pay (1–10)**: Existing budget line items vs discretionary spend.
5. **AI-Agent Suitability (1–10)**: Workflow need for multi-step reasoning, document extraction, and cross-system action.
6. **Automation Potential (1–10)**: Percentage of deterministic/repetitive tasks delegable to agents.
7. **Existing Competition (1–10)**: Favorable score (10 = no direct agentic competition / fragmented market).
8. **Competitive Whitespace (1–10)**: Opportunity created specifically by modern LLM/Agent state machines.
9. **Ease of Reaching First Customers (1–10)**: Access to economic buyers without 18-month RFP cycles.
10. **Implementation Complexity (1–10)**: Favorable score (10 = buildable in weeks/months with modern stacks).
11. **Regulatory Risk (1–10)**: Favorable score (10 = manageable FERPA/COPPA/privacy controls).
12. **Data/Access Difficulty (1–10)**: Favorable score (10 = accessible inputs like PDFs, emails, forms, standard APIs).
13. **Global Scalability (1–10)**: Transferability from US market to UK, Canada, Australia, EU, GCC.
14. **Potential Recurring Revenue (1–10)**: High ARR capacity per account (\$10k–\$100k+).

## 🎯 Top 3 Recommended Business Builds for a 1–5 Person AI Systems Company

1. **Automated Higher Education Transfer Credit & Articulation Agent**
   - *Target:* Community Colleges, State Universities, Online Universities.
   - *Model:* Service-to-Product (\$15k setup + \$2k/mo SaaS per campus).
   - *Core Value:* Reduces evaluation time from 4 weeks to 2 minutes, preventing student enrollment melt.

2. **K-12 Special Education (IEP/504) Compliance & Audit Agent**
   - *Target:* Mid-sized School Districts (3,000–25,000 students), Special Ed Directors.
   - *Model:* Annual SaaS contract (\$12k–\$35k/year per district).
   - *Core Value:* Automates accommodations auditing, progress report compilation, and legal error checking to prevent IDEA due process lawsuits (\$23k+ avg settlement).

3. **Financial Aid ISIR & Verification Exception Orchestration Agent**
   - *Target:* Higher Education Financial Aid Offices (Community colleges & public universities).
   - *Model:* Usage + SaaS tier (\$15k–\$45k/year).
   - *Core Value:* Eliminates manual tax/W-2/household audit bottlenecks, saving 20%+ of financial aid operating budgets and boosting matriculation.
