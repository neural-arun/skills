## 1. Problem & scope

Scale is verified and enormous: ~15% of US students were chronically absent pre-pandemic → 31% in 2021-22 → 28% in 2022-23 ([US ED](https://www.ed.gov/teaching-and-administration/supporting-numbers/supporting-students/chronic-absenteeism) and [ed.gov page](https://www.ed.gov/teaching-and-administration/supporting-students/chronic-absenteeism), **VERIFIED**; carried from raw file). AEI's tracker puts the 41-state average at **23.5% in 2024**, down from the 28% peak ([AEI via The74](https://www.the74million.org/article/iowa-declared-war-on-chronic-absenteeism-now-its-gaining-some-ground/), **VERIFIED**) — improving, but no state is back to pre-pandemic levels ([FutureEd via Stateline/The74](https://www.the74million.org/article/states-try-new-measures-to-get-chronically-absent-students-back-to-class/), **VERIFIED**). Even improved, ~11M+ students/yr are chronically absent (**ESTIMATE** from 23.5% × ~49M enrollment). Attendance Works frames the cause taxonomy: **barriers, aversion, disengagement, misconceptions** — explicitly warning against assuming truancy or parental indifference ([Attendance Works root-causes page](https://www.attendanceworks.org/chronic-absence/addressing-chronic-absence/3-tiers-of-intervention/root-causes/), **VERIFIED**), and noting poverty doubles/triples chronic-absence odds ([the-problem page](https://www.attendanceworks.org/chronic-absence/the-problem/), **VERIFIED**).

The regulatory ground is shifting fast, and it is shifting toward *mandated workflow artifacts* — the exact things this agent produces:

- **Iowa SF 2435 (2024)**: notify parents at 10% non-exempt absences of a grading period → **mandatory attendance plan at 15%** when progress is affected → **county-attorney referral at 20%**; standardized absence-exemption coding statewide ([SF 2435 bill book](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/legislation/BillBook?ga=90&ba=sf2435), [IA DOE guidance](https://educate.iowa.gov/pk-12/student-supports/prevention/attendance-chronic-absenteeism#attendance-coding-technical-assistance), **VERIFIED** via [The74 Iowa article](https://www.the74million.org/article/iowa-declared-war-on-chronic-absenteeism-now-its-gaining-some-ground/)). Statewide chronic absence fell 21.6% → <16% in one year; Des Moines 45.4%→21.2%; Cedar Rapids 38.6%→27.8%; Davenport 42.5%→33.2% (**VERIFIED**, same source; causal attribution partly confounded — see §11).
- **2026 session**: **70+ bills across 24 states + DC** ([FutureEd tracker](https://www.future-ed.org/legislative-tracker-2026-state-chronic-absenteeism-bills/), **VERIFIED**, updated 7/13/2026). Enacted: **Utah SB58** (state monitoring system; notice-of-violation after 5 truancies; class-B misdemeanor path for non-engaging parents), **Oregon HB4154** (public attendance-data reporting), **Vermont H.930** (truancy = 20 unexcused; $1,000 fines/court petitions only after documented school-based interventions — i.e., *intervention documentation becomes a legal precondition*), **Tennessee SB1968** (absence records follow transfers; withdrawing during Tier-3 intervention reported to juvenile court), **Mississippi SB2103** (tiered intervention mandate + **state-funded attendance officer per 4,000 students** — a new funded role that needs tooling), **Maryland HB575**, **NJ A1733** task force (**all VERIFIED**, same tracker + [Stateline article](https://www.the74million.org/article/states-try-new-measures-to-get-chronically-absent-students-back-to-class/)). Moving bills: NJ S294 and NY S8528 **attendance review teams**, GA SB513 "Every Day Counts Act," NY A10239 (early-warning systems + review teams + arrest prohibition) (**VERIFIED**, tracker).
- Accountability ties: **36 states + DC adopted chronic absence in ESSA accountability** (2018 wave) ([Attendance Works state policy page](https://www.attendanceworks.org/policy/state-education-policy/), **VERIFIED**); Iowa made it an accountability metric 2023-24 (**VERIFIED**, The74 Iowa). Counter-trend watch: Oklahoma moved to stop counting it against schools in 2026 (The74 related-link headline observed this session, body not fetched — **UNVERIFIED detail**).

**In scope (v1 hypothesis):** trigger→investigate→outreach→plan assembly→coordination→verification→escalation packets→extracts, for one state's statutory format.
**Out of scope initially:** Tier-1 schoolwide campaigns (incumbents own this), transportation operations itself, health-clinical workflows, court representation.

## 2. Workflow today + failure modes

Reconstructed from raw-file problem #4, Attendance Works 3-tier model (**VERIFIED** framework), Iowa statute mechanics (**VERIFIED**), and practitioner voices (theme 6):

1. Teachers take daily attendance → SIS aggregates → weekly at-risk lists (approaching 5%/10% thresholds).
2. Clerk/principal sends state-mandated form letters at defined thresholds; calls home; logs attempts.
3. Attendance team / student-success meetings assign Tier-2 supports; someone writes an attendance contract or success plan (often on paper or Google Docs).
4. Coordination with counselors/social workers/community agencies happens by email and hallway conversation.
5. At statutory ceilings: SARB (CA) / attendance review team / county-attorney referral packet assembled manually from whatever documentation exists.
6. Year-end reconciliation for state chronic-absence reporting (ESSA public reporting everywhere).

**Failure modes (each maps to a product surface):**

- **No investigation:** nobody asks *why* before escalating; form letters assume indifference. Attendance Works explicitly warns this assumption is wrong and that root-cause confirmation requires talking with families ([root-causes page](https://www.attendanceworks.org/chronic-absence/addressing-chronic-absence/3-tiers-of-intervention/root-causes/), **VERIFIED**).
- **Contact-data rot:** "most of the students' parents had no phone or email contact information" — the 1,114-upvote r/Teachers complaint ([practitioner-voices theme 6](https://www.reddit.com/r/Teachers/comments/1nc4zr6/yes_we_are_all_tired_of_calling_parents_but_i/), **VERIFIED thread**). Outreach fails silently; nobody fixes the record.
- **Documentation debt:** Iowa now *requires* a plan at 15%; Vermont requires documented interventions before court action (**VERIFIED** statutes above). Districts generate these by hand, inconsistently — creating both labor burden and legal exposure when packets are thin.
- **Escalation roulette:** whether a family reaches the county attorney depends on which clerk handled the file ("We refer when they won't engage… It's never a surprise to any family" — Des Moines, 80 referrals/yr; "The punitive approach doesn't seem to work for us ever" — Cedar Rapids; both **VERIFIED**, The74 Iowa). Unequal administration = equity/legal risk.
- **Capacity collapse:** clerks cut post-ESSER (raw file, **VERIFIED** hiring context); Des Moines fields **46 case managers** for 29,700 students (**VERIFIED**, The74 Iowa) — most districts have nothing close.
- **Data-quality chaos:** GAO/ED flag attendance data caution; measurement disagreement is itself a 2026 news story ([The74](https://www.the74million.org/article/absenteeism-is-a-major-problem-so-why-cant-schools-agree-on-how-to-measure-it/), **VERIFIED** existence; carried in landscape §1.6). Duplicate student records corrupt rates (raw file cross-cutting #5).

## 5. Competitive teardown

**EveryDay Labs** — the strongest incumbent. Assets: behavioral-science pedigree (Rogers/Feller/Harvard lineage studies on its research page, **VERIFIED listings**), **ESSA Strong Evidence badge via Johns Hopkins Evidence for ESSA** (marketing claim referencing third-party standard, **VENDOR-CLAIM** pending the underlying RCT read), DC RCT Spring 2025 + Louisiana study (**VENDOR-CLAIM**, PDFs gated), 24/7 multilingual family-support bot *and human team*, big-name district logos (Houston, DCPS, Atlanta, Sacramento City — **VERIFIED** homepage). **Loop closure: partial.** They detect, message, triage inbound family requests ("barriers & notes logged"), and coordinate intervention notes — but there is no evidence of: cross-system root-cause investigation, statutory plan/referral document assembly, escalation workflow, or state-extract production. Their pricing also reveals the margin structure: humans (Family Support Team) + print/mail logistics. Consolidation signal: EDL site footer links Terms/Privacy to **newsela.com** legal pages (observed **VERIFIED** on-site; acquisition press release not found this session → ownership status **UNKNOWN**, consolidation trajectory clear).

**SchoolStatus Attend** — fastest-moving threat. Vendor-run analysis of 89 customer districts/513K students across 9 states: −18% chronic absence year one, −36% cumulative by year two, 96% of districts improved (**VENDOR-CLAIM** — self-selected cohort, no controls, state-reported denominators; directionally credible). Product already includes early-warning at 60 instructional days, letters/postcards, two-way translation texting. Critically, for 2026-27 they are **launching daily same-day absence notifications with a family-facing absence-reason capture** plus time-based attendance tracking (**VERIFIED**, [press/blog](https://www.schoolstatus.com/blog/new-data-shows-how-89-districts-reduced-chronic-absenteeism)) — i.e., they are marching straight into the investigation layer's first mile. What they visibly do NOT ship: statutory attendance-plan assembly, SARB/court escalation packets, multi-system (transport/health/discipline) synthesis, state extracts.

**ParentSquare (+Remind)** — owns the broadcast channel; absence-reporting (SafeArrival-style) covers morning check-in, not intervention loops (landscape §1.6 **FACT** existence / **INFER** gap). Not a threat to documentation depth; potential acquirer of whoever wins it.

**Panorama / Branching Minds (MTSS)** — store plans and intervention logs; Iowa bought Panorama statewide (**VERIFIED**, The74 Iowa). They are systems-of-record for interventions, not executors; integration partner rather than competitor (agent writes plans *into* them).

**Why the gap persists:** (1) investigation requires joining attendance+transport+health+discipline silos behind weak SIS APIs (landscape §3 integration-debt analysis); (2) statutory documentation is state-specific, low-glamour maintenance treadmill incumbents won't staff; (3) escalation touches courts → liability aversion; (4) incumbents monetize broadcast volume and human support minutes, not per-case closure; (5) until 2024-26, few statutes *forced* artifact production — now they do.

**Incumbent-response risk: HIGH on outreach features, MODERATE on documentation, LOW on escalation.** SchoolStatus will ship reason-code capture; EDL will bolt AI onto its bot. Neither has shown appetite for court-adjacent workflows or per-state legal templates. The durable wedge is the *legally load-bearing* layer.

## 6. Technical feasibility (1–5 people)

**SIS attendance API reality:** PowerSchool exposes REST APIs/plugin framework with a large partner marketplace (marketplace **FACT** carried from landscape §1.1; current API depth **UNKNOWN** — developer portal unreachable this session). Infinite Campus: web-services API historically gated to district-enabled access (**ESTIMATE** from landscape INFER). Skyward: thin partner API surface (**INFER** carried). Practical answer for a tiny team: **don't integrate first — ingest.** Attendance data is structurally trivial (student × day × code); nightly CSV/SFTP extracts or scheduled state-report exports cover v1 for any SIS. OneRoster where available. Reserve API work for write-back (contact updates, plan status) in phase 2.

**Root-cause joining quality:** attendance + contacts + discipline + enrollment history join cleanly on state IDs (**ESTIMATE** — duplicates are the failure mode; raw file flags duplicate-record cleanup as deterministic agent work). Transport lives in separate systems (Versatrans/Traversa-class; bus-scan exports exist in some fleets — **UNKNOWN** prevalence). Health data is the minefield: nurse logs sit in restricted SIS health modules with heightened sensitivity; **v1 should not scrape health records** — instead render staff-entered flags (asthma plan? chronic illness?) into the investigation brief. McKinney-Vento/foster flags: staff-attested booleans, not inferred. Expect the investigation brief to be 70% auto-synthesized + 30% staff-completed — that ratio is honest and defensible (**DESIGN ESTIMATE**).

**Multilingual voice/SMS agents:** commodity stack (Twilio + modern speech models + LLM dialogue); EDL proves 75-language outbound is table stakes (**VERIFIED** feature). Differentiators worth building: dialect/formality tuning, inbound comprehension with structured intent capture (the "why was your child absent" reason taxonomy feeding root-cause stats — SchoolStatus validates demand), consent/opt-out ledger, and full transcript logging for audit.

**Documentation templates by state:** genuinely tractable per-state LLM work — Iowa's 10%/15%/20% ladder artifacts, CA SARB forms (SARB process specifics: district SARB → COE-level appeal, EC 48260-series referrals — **ESTIMATE/domain-knowledge, CDE blocked this session, verify before CA entry**), Vermont's intervention-before-petition evidentiary trail. Budget ~2–4 engineer-weeks per state for template + validation harness (**ESTIMATE**).

**v1 scope recommendation:** **One state (Iowa), SIS-agnostic CSV ingestion, four artifacts** (threshold letters, 15% attendance plan, 20% county-attorney referral packet, state extract) **plus SMS-first outreach with human-approved voice escalation.** Skip health scraping, skip API write-back, skip voice-inbound until contract #3.

**Hardest three risks:** (1) **Data hygiene** — duplicate/stale records poison both outreach and rates (practitioner-voices theme 6 is literally a data-hygiene complaint); make record-repair a visible deliverable, not plumbing. (2) **Sensitive-contact misfires** — see §7; one bad call can end the company locally. (3) **Attribution** — proving YOUR delta vs nudges-vs-seasonality (§8/§11).

## 7. Regulatory / deployment

- **FERPA:** vendor qualifies as school official under direct-control exception with a signed data-use agreement; least-privilege architecture is now a selling post-PowerSchool-breach (landscape Cautionary Tale #2, **VERIFIED** events). Investigation briefs aggregate sensitive categories (discipline, disability-adjacent flags) — enforce purpose limitation per role.
- **COPPA:** applies to under-13 contexts; district consent covers educational-purpose collection, but the agent communicates with **parents**, not children — keep the minor out of any conversational loop entirely (design rule, **ESTIMATE** conservative reading; confirm with counsel).
- **TCPA/consent for SMS/voice:** school-function communications enjoy narrower constraints than marketing, but automated voice to wireless numbers is the sharp edge; implement carrier-grade opt-out, quiet-hours logic, and per-guardian consent ledgers (**ESTIMATE** — obtain a formal TCPA opinion before voice launch; **UNKNOWN** how districts' existing ParentSquare/Remind consents transfer).
- **When the agent must NOT contact (hard guardrails, product-level):** active DV order/custody restriction on a guardian (contact-flag table fed by registrar), open CPS/welfare proceeding flagged by social worker (route to human only), McKinney-Vento families in crisis (outreach only via designated liaison), bereavement/hospitalization codes, any health/disability-specific discussion (human-only topic gate), and always: **no AI-initiated communication that references court action** — referral communications are drafted by the agent, sent by a named human (**DESIGN RULE**; grounded in Vermont's intervene-before-court sequencing and Iowa's prosecutor-discretion reality, **VERIFIED** statutes).
- **Court-document standards:** referral packets feed county-attorney/juvenile dockets → certified contact-attempt logs with timestamps, staff-signed attestations, immutable audit trail, state retention-schedule compliance (**ESTIMATE** of practice; exact evidentiary standards per county **UNKNOWN** — validate with 2-3 county attorneys during Iowa design partner phase).
- **State privacy regimes:** student-DPA requirements and laws like SOPIPA/NY EdLaw 2d-class statutes apply (**ESTIMATE** general; per-state matrix needed at expansion time). SOC 2 before any >10K-student deal.

## 11. Kill risks (top 5) + falsification tests

1. **"Nudges already capture most of the value."** EDL/SchoolStatus messaging is cheap, proven (EDL ESSA-strong badge; SchoolStatus 89-district claim — both **VENDOR-CLAIM**-grade), and installed widely. If messaging exhausts the tractable fraction of absence reduction, the investigation/documentation layer fights for scraps. **Test:** in pilot districts already running EDL/SchoolStatus, measure Tier-3 case-resolution rate and 15%-plan compliance BEFORE/AFTER our layer; if our delta on top of mature nudge programs is <2 pts chronic absence or statutorily-required artifacts remain >80% hand-made anyway (i.e., districts shrug), the wedge is decorative → kill or pivot to pure document-engine utility pricing.
2. **"It's poverty, not process."** AW's own framing: poverty doubles/triples risk (**VERIFIED**); CT home-visit gains show relationship/services beat letters (**VERIFIED** CCERC finding). If ≥70% of a pilot district's chronic absence decomposes to housing instability/unmet health needs that require services the district doesn't possess, the agent becomes a referral-list writer. **Test:** run the reason-taxonomy capture for one semester; if solvable-by-school-process categories (misconceptions, scheduling, transport-fixable, documentation) <40% of case volume, cap ambition at concierge triage pricing or exit.
3. **Measurement chaos destroys the ROI story.** The field literally cannot agree how to measure absence ([The74, Aug 2026](https://www.the74million.org/article/absenteeism-is-a-major-problem-so-why-cant-schools-agree-on-how-to-measure-it/), **VERIFIED**); state data lags a year; Oklahoma is retreating from accountability counting (**UNVERIFIED** detail). Without agreed endpoints, renewal fights every June. **Test:** pre-registered matched-design agreements signed by 3 consecutive buyers; refusal rate >50% at proposal stage = market isn't ready for outcome-based claims → retreat to labor-savings pricing (weaker but survivable) or walk.
4. **Incumbent speed.** SchoolStatus's 2026-27 daily-notification + reason-capture release (**VERIFIED**) shows they iterate fast toward investigation; EDL has capital via Newsela consolidation (ownership **UNKNOWN** but signals point that way). If either ships per-state statutory document packs within ~18 months, the wedge collapses to service margins. **Test:** quarterly teardown of their release notes + win/loss logging on every deal where "they announced that" appears; two such losses in a row = compress roadmap to escalation/legal layer only.
5. **A single catastrophic contact event.** AI voice/SMS reaching a protected-guardian household (DV order, custody dispute, fatality) triggers local-news coverage; post-AllHere, districts execute on trust violations instantly (landscape Cautionary Tales #1, **VERIFIED** arc). **Test/guardrail:** ship the do-not-contact registry + human-send gate on ALL court-adjacent language in contract #1; tabletop the incident with each buyer; any guardrail bypass found in audits = freeze outbound, fix, disclose.

## 12. Verdict: **BUILD-CAREFULLY**

This is a real, well-timed, structurally attractive wedge sitting next to a crowded one. The honest paragraph: the *messaging* layer everyone thinks of as "the attendance product" is commoditized, publicly priced at $5–$9.75/student, and owned by two competent incumbents — entering there would be suicide. What changed in 2024–26 is that states started legislating the *unautomated* parts: Iowa forces attendance plans at 15% and prosecutor referrals at 20%; Vermont makes documented intervention a legal precondition to petitioning; Mississippi funds attendance officers; 70+ bills are live. Those statutes convert attendance from a persuasion problem into a **document-production and case-management problem** — precisely LLM-agent territory and precisely where incumbents have shown no appetite because it's per-state grind with courtroom adjacency. The bear case is equally honest: absence is substantially a poverty phenomenon, nudges demonstrably move total absences only a few percent, the field can't agree on measurement, and SchoolStatus is visibly marching toward the investigation layer's first mile with daily reason-capture.
