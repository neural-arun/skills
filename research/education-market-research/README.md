## One-paragraph answer

Education's unsolved pain is a single repeating shape: regulated document pipelines between humans, agencies, and legacy systems that nobody owns end-to-end. The strongest opportunities found: **#1 school Medicaid billing capture** (self-funding, CFO buyer, auditor-endorsed problem), **#2 transfer/transcript intelligence** (GAO-verified 43% credit loss), **#3 certification-body adjudication ops** (fastest sales cycles, no fatal flaw), **#4 SPED casework copilot** (loudest verified pain; UK expansion), **#5 career-college filing factory** (owner signs in one call). Red team killed nothing outright but weakened everything: **all windows are 12–18 months** before incumbent bundling closes them, pilot→paid conversion is the untested assumption under every TAM figure, and hours×rate ROI math that never cashes out is the most common failure pattern. Recommended next step: three <$10K falsification tests in parallel (Illinois Medicaid capture, cert-body config gate, stop-out contactability audit) — then concentrate ruthlessly.
