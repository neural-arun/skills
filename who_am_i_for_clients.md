# WHO AM I?

# AI Systems Architect for Healthcare & Educational Platforms

I help healthcare and education organizations turn **manual, fragmented workflows into reliable software systems combining AI, data, automation, and human expertise.**

I focus on problems where teams spend significant time **collecting information, analyzing it, making decisions, communicating, and following up manually.**

Rather than building isolated AI features, I design the **complete workflow around the AI.**

## Problems I Want to Solve

### Healthcare

* Patient intake and information collection
* Patient monitoring and follow-up
* Red-flag identification and escalation
* Clinical and operational information retrieval
* Administrative workflow automation
* Patient communication and reminders
* Converting unstructured information into structured data

**Goal:** Help healthcare professionals access the right information at the right time, reduce repetitive work, and make better-informed decisions.

### Education & Coaching

* Student assessment and performance analysis
* Personalized tests and learning plans based on performance
* Automated progress tracking
* Mentor/teacher dashboards
* Student follow-up and engagement
* Verified AI-generated questions and content
* Learning analytics and intervention

**Goal:** Help educators and coaches personalize support and serve more students without proportionally increasing administrative workload.

## How I Build These Systems

I think about every solution as a complete chain:

**Problem → Workflow → Data → Software → AI & Automation → Human Verification → Interface → Deployment → Measurement**

My process is:

**1. Understand** — Learn how the organization actually operates.
**2. Identify** — Find expensive, repetitive, error-prone, or hard-to-scale bottlenecks.
**3. Design** — Define the data flow, architecture, AI, automation, and human checkpoints.
**4. Build** — Develop and integrate the system with existing tools and workflows.
**5. Verify** — Add appropriate validation, safeguards, and human oversight.
**6. Improve** — Measure real-world results and continuously iterate.
